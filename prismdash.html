Prism Dash

Chain gems. Bank your pot. Don't crash. A roguelike runner where every lane is a gamble.

Become aBeta tester How it plays

Free on Android, with iOS on the way. No in-app purchases.

One swipe from glory

Every run is a push-your-luck race. The bigger your pot gets, the more a single wall costs you.

Chain

Grab the same gem again and again to grow your streak. Each gem adds its value times your streak to the pot.

Bank

Swipe down to lock the pot into your score, or keep chaining for more. Hit a wall and the whole pot is gone.

Build

Spend coins at the Pit Stop on trinkets that bend the rules, then stack them into ridiculous combos.

Never the same run twice

Roguelike choices on top of a three-lane runner.

Dozens of trinkets

Hot Streak, Crown Jewels, Mirror Ball and more, each one changing how you score.

Rare editions

Foil, Holo and Prismatic gems supercharge your streak when they show up.

Gate choices

Pick a lane at every gate for coins, shields, focus or a bigger pot multiplier.

Stardust progress

Every run earns Stardust, even a crash, for permanent upgrades and unlocks.

Five Heat levels

Beat a route, then turn up the Heat for tougher runs and bigger rewards.

Cloud saves

Progress backs up automatically. Link Google Play Games to carry it to a new phone.
