Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Prism Dash ("Prism Dash," "we," "us," or "our"), a mobile game developed by Isaac Mayernik, collects, uses, shares, and protects information about people who play Prism Dash on iOS and Android (the "Game"). By playing the Game, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

Summary

  • You can play without creating an account or giving us your name or email address.
  • The Game backs up your progress (such as Stardust, unlocks, and statistics) to the cloud using Unity Gaming Services, tied to an anonymous player ID.
  • You can optionally sign in with Google Play Games (Android) or Sign in with Apple (iOS) to keep your progress across devices.
  • We do not show advertising, sell personal information, offer in-app purchases, or use third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • You can delete your account and your online data at any time from within the Game.

1. Information we collect

Anonymous player ID
When you start the Game, Unity Authentication creates an anonymous player ID and a randomly generated player name (for example, "NobleKnittingApron"). This ID is not linked to your real name or email address.
Game progress
Your Stardust, upgrades, unlocked trinkets, runners and routes, completed goals, game statistics (such as runs played, wins, best zone, best bank, and best streak), and game settings. This is saved to Unity Cloud Save under your player ID so it can be restored.
Sign-in account (optional)
If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games or Apple, we receive an identifier for that account so we can link it to your player ID. We do not receive your password, and we do not request your email address or contacts.
Technical information
When the Game connects to Unity Gaming Services, Unity automatically receives technical information needed to provide the service, such as your IP address, device model, operating system, and Game version.

We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, payment information, or the contents of your device. A run in progress is saved only on your device.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Save your progress and restore it on this or another device;
  • Link an optional Google Play Games or Apple account to your progress;
  • Keep the Game's online services working and secure; and
  • Respond to your questions and requests.

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising or profiling.

3. How we share information

Service providers. We use Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication and Unity Cloud Save), provided by Unity Technologies, to create anonymous player IDs and store game progress. Unity processes this information on our behalf in accordance with its privacy policy.

Sign-in providers. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games or Apple, that provider processes your sign-in under its own privacy policy (Google, Apple).

Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of our players or others.

We do not otherwise share, sell, or rent personal information to third parties.

4. Information stored on your device

The Game stores your progress, any run in progress, and your settings on your device so it works offline. Uninstalling the Game removes this information from your device.

5. Device permissions

The Game uses an internet connection to back up your progress and may use vibration for feedback (you can turn vibration off in Settings). It does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos.

6. Data security

Information sent between the Game and Unity Gaming Services is encrypted in transit. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your player ID and game progress for as long as your account exists.

Deleting your account. In the Game, go to Settings > Delete account. This permanently deletes your player account and your saved progress from Unity Gaming Services and removes your progress from your device.

Resetting progress. Settings > Reset progress erases your progress on your device and overwrites your cloud backup, while keeping your player account.

If you cannot access the Game, you can request deletion by emailing us at the address below with the subject "Prism Dash data deletion" and your player name (shown in Settings > Account). We respond to requests within 3 to 5 business days.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Play anonymously. Signing in with Google Play Games or Apple is optional.
  • Access. You can request a copy of the information associated with your player ID by contacting us.
  • Deletion. You may delete your account or reset your progress as described in Section 7.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law, such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation or the California Consumer Privacy Act. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

9. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. If we make material changes, we will also provide notice in the Game.
