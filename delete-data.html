Privacy Policy

Delete your Liftly data

You can delete your workout data, or request deletion of specific information, without deleting your account. To delete everything, see Delete your account.

Reset your data in the App

This is the fastest option, and your data is deleted immediately.

  1. Open Liftly and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Settings (the gear icon).
  3. Scroll to the Other section and tap Reset All Data.
  4. Confirm the reset.

What Reset All Data deletes

The following is permanently removed from our servers and your device:

  • Workout history
  • Saved routines
  • Goals
  • Personal records and workout totals
  • Reactions on your workouts

What it keeps

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Your profile: name, username, profile photo, and body measurements
  • Your friends, friend requests, and blocked users
  • Reactions you have sent to others
  • Preferences saved to your account

Deleted data cannot be recovered. You can continue logging new workouts right away.

Request deletion of specific data

To delete information that Reset All Data keeps, such as your profile photo or body measurements, or if you can't access the App, submit this form. It opens a pre-filled email in your mail app for you to send. To help us verify your request, please send it from the email address associated with your account.

We respond to deletion requests within 3 to 5 business days.

For more about how we handle your information, read our Privacy Policy.
