Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Liftly ("Liftly," "we," "us," or "our"), a mobile application developed by Isaac Mayernik, collects, uses, shares, and protects information about people who use the Liftly app for iOS and Android (the "App"). By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

Summary

  • The App can be used without an account. In that case, the information you enter is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.
  • If you create an account, your information is stored using Google Firebase to provide syncing and social features.
  • Your workouts are visible only to users you have accepted as friends. Your goals, body measurements, routines, and personal records are not displayed to other users.
  • We do not sell personal information, show advertising, or use third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • You may delete your account and its associated data at any time from within the App.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Use without an account

If you use the App without an account, your workouts, routines, goals, body measurements, and settings are stored only on your device. We do not receive or store this information.

1.2 Use with an account

If you create an account, we collect the following categories of information:

Account credentials
Your email address and password. These are managed by Firebase Authentication; we cannot view your password.
Profile information
Your first and last name, username, optional profile photo, account creation date, and most recent sign-in time.
Workout data
Exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, workout dates, saved routines, personal records, and workout totals.
Goals and body measurements
If you set goals: your goal type, current and target body weight, activity level, age, height, sex, and calculated calorie targets.
Social activity
Friend requests, your friends list, users you have blocked, and reactions you send and receive.
Preferences
App settings saved to your account, such as muscle group colors.
Usage information
The most recent day you used the App, your device platform (iOS or Android), and the App version. This is used only to measure the number of active users.

We do not collect location data, contacts, advertising identifiers, or crash reports, and we do not use third-party analytics services.

2. Information visible to other users

  • All signed-in users can search for you by name or username and view your name, username, profile photo, and workout totals.
  • Users you have accepted as friends can also view your workouts, react to them, and see reactions on them.
  • Not displayed to other users: your email address, goals, body measurements, routines, personal records, preferences, and blocked users list.

If you block a user, they are removed from your friends list, you are removed from theirs, and any pending friend requests between you are cancelled.

3. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the App's features, including workout logging, syncing across devices, progress tracking, and goal and calorie calculations;
  • Provide social features, including friend requests, the activity feed, and reactions;
  • Maintain the safety and integrity of the App, including filtering inappropriate usernames and preventing misuse;
  • Measure the number of active users; and
  • Respond to your questions and requests.

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

4. How we share information

Service providers. We use Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to authenticate users and store account data. Google processes this information on our behalf in accordance with its privacy and security terms.

Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users or others.

We do not otherwise share, sell, or rent personal information to third parties.

5. Information stored on your device

The App stores certain information on your device so that it works quickly and offline. This includes a cached copy of your workouts, your sign-in session, App settings, and notification preferences. If you use the App without an account, all of your data is stored this way. Uninstalling the App removes this information from your device.

6. Device permissions

Photos
Requested only if you choose to set a profile photo. The App accesses only the photo you select.
Notifications
If you allow notifications, the App sends motivational notifications. These are scheduled on your device; we do not collect a notification token or any other information to send them.

You can change either permission at any time in your device's settings.

7. Data security

Information transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit. Access rules on our database restrict who can read and modify each category of information, as described in Section 2. While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Data retention and deletion

We retain account information for as long as your account exists.

Deleting your account. You can delete your account in the App under Profile > Settings > Delete Account. Deleting your account permanently removes your account credentials, profile information, workout data, goals and body measurements, social activity, preferences, and usage information from our servers, and removes you from other users' friends lists. A copy of your workouts remains on your device so that you can continue using the App without an account; uninstalling the App removes it.

Deleting data only. You can delete your workout data while keeping your account using Profile > Settings > Reset All Data.

If you cannot access the App, you may request deletion of your account or request deletion of specific data. We respond to requests within 3 to 5 business days.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Use without an account. You may use the App without sending any information to us.
  • Access and correction. You can view and update your profile, goals, and body measurements in the App. You may also request a copy of your information, or ask us to correct it, by contacting us.
  • Control over visibility. You decide which friend requests to accept, and you can remove friends or block users at any time.
  • Deletion. You may delete your account or your data as described in Section 8.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable law, such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation or the California Consumer Privacy Act. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

10. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. If we make material changes, we will also provide notice in the App.
