Privacy Policy

Delete your Liftly account

You can delete your account and the data associated with it at any time, either in the App or by submitting a request below.

Account deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted, it cannot be recovered and you will no longer be able to sign in with it.

Delete your account in the App

This is the fastest option, and your account is deleted immediately.

  1. Open Liftly and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Settings (the gear icon).
  3. Scroll to the Other section and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

What is deleted

The following information is permanently removed from our servers:

Account
Your sign-in credentials and email address.
Profile
Your name, username, profile photo, and body measurements.
Workout data
Workout history, routines, goals, personal records, and workout totals.
Social activity
Your friends (you are also removed from their friends lists), friend requests, reactions you sent and received, and your blocked users list.
Other
Preferences saved to your account and your usage record.

What stays on your device

When you delete your account in the App, a copy of your workouts is kept on your device so you can continue using Liftly without an account. This copy is never sent to us. To remove it, uninstall the App or use Reset All Data in Settings before deleting your account.

Request deletion by email

If you can't access the App, submit this form. It opens a pre-filled email in your mail app for you to send. To help us verify your request, please send it from the email address associated with your account.

We respond to deletion requests within 3 to 5 business days.

If you only want to remove some of your data and keep your account, see Delete your data. For more about how we handle your information, read our Privacy Policy.
